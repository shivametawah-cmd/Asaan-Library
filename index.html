Backup your data

Download everything in your library as one Excel file: students (active and inactive), the recycle bin, payments, expenses and approval requests. Save it somewhere safe, for example Google Drive or a pen drive. It contains mobile numbers, so do not share it.

When this is on, the backup downloads by itself the first time you open Asaan Library after 7 days. It cannot run while the app is closed. Turn it on separately on each phone or computer you use. Your browser may ask permission to download files.

Restoring from a backup

The first sheet (Students) uses the same columns as the import template. To bring students back, go to Students, choose the backup file under Import, and tick "Update existing" if you also want to correct details (up to 450 rows at a time). Payments, expenses and requests are kept in the file for your records.

Recycle bin

Deleted students are kept here with all their details. Restore brings a student back exactly as they were, and their earlier payments stay linked. Delete forever cannot be undone.
